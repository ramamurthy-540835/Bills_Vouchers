# Compliance calendar requirements

Deadlines require versioned taxpayer/form/period/state/frequency rules and
notification evidence, including extensions and waivers. Reference dates in
existing UI are not a complete statutory deadline engine.

Required states: upcoming, due today, overdue, filed, not applicable and blocked.
Filed requires acknowledgement evidence. Reminders require functioning delivery
and user authorization. No immutable universal 11th/13th/20th/22nd/24th schedule
is introduced by V3.

Status: effective-date deadline engine and reminder scheduling NOT_IMPLEMENTED.
