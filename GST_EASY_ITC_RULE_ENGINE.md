# ITC professional review scope

Review CGST sections 16–18 and applicable Rules 36, 37, 37A, 38–44 with effective
dates and scenario evidence. Separate eligible, blocked, pending evidence,
pending reconciliation, reversed, reclaimable, reclaimed and time-barred states.

Do not infer eligibility from product names, active GSTIN, statement import or
arithmetic match alone. Track tax heads and opening/new/reversal/reclaim/
utilization/closing balances only when authoritative evidence exists.

Existing supported deterministic calculations remain unchanged. A complete
effective-date statutory ITC engine and ledger reconciliation are not certified.
