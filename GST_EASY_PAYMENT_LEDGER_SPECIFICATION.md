# Payment and ledger requirements

Separate electronic cash, credit and liability evidence. Entries need registration,
period, tax head, source reference, amount, effective date and immutable audit.
Opening and closing balances must reconcile; reversals/reclaims are separate
from utilization. Apply only professionally verified effective-period set-off
restrictions across IGST, CGST, SGST and cess.

Current cash estimates are not authoritative government balances. Challan creation,
payment confirmation, liability offset and refunds are not integrated. Never
mark a payment successful without its verified acknowledgement.
