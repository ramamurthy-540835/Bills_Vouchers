# Nature Labs GST Easy V2 guide

Demo email: naturelabs@demo.com. Password: owner-managed Secret Manager only; never in this document. The initial requested password does not meet the unchanged policy. Use at least 12 characters, uppercase/lowercase and a number, maximum 72 UTF-8 bytes.

Three planned profiles: Nature Labs Demo Manufacturing Pvt Ltd (Chennai), Nature Labs Demo Trading Pvt Ltd (Coimbatore), Nature Labs Demo Services Pvt Ltd (Bengaluru). All are synthetic, not government-verified. One login has owner membership only; the display name containing Administrator does not grant platform administration.

After provisioning, use the top-right Switch business selector or Business Profile > My Businesses. Check the selected business before uploading. Profiles have fictional contact/address details and individual logos.

Dashboards start without uploaded financial records. Select October 2026. From demo bills/, choose the selected business folder and upload ONE representation per invoice (PDF OR PNG OR JPG). Alternate files represent the same invoice and should be detected as business duplicates. Review supplier/recipient, direction, dates, tax heads and totals. Confirm only valid records. Check the expected workbook for signed purchase/sales values; ITC is not automatically approved.

Import the matching business file from gstr2b/ using Returns & Credit. Only supported B2B sections are present. Matching does not establish ITC eligibility. Duplicate statements in edge_cases are intentional rejection tests. Do not use these documents for tax filing or real business records.

All nine report views are read-only in V2. Restricted financial exports and filing stay disabled for synthetic tenants. Test return preparation may also be restricted by existing synthetic-workspace policy. Missing statement and unavailable calculations must not be presented as genuine zeros.

Mobile/SMS, automatic government verification and email recovery require real providers. Demo operator activation is not email or GST ownership verification. Never share credentials or upload real customer data into this public demo.
