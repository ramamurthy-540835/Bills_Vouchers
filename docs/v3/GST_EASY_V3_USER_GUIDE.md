# V3 engineering preview guide

Register a business using an email address you control. Open the verification
link within 30 minutes, then sign in. Resend or recovery requests do not reveal
whether an account exists. Do not share passwords or verification links.

Use the account menu for Business Profile, Add Business and Switch Business.
Confirm the selected business and period before uploading or reviewing records.
Upload Bills remains on GST Easy and Bills & Vouchers; statement imports belong
in Returns & Credit.

In bill review, compare recorded GST with declared-rate arithmetic. Open the
authorized original to verify facts. The arithmetic display is not legal rate
verification. Select a rejection category and enter a reason when required.
For a rejected bill, request correction, edit/save, then resubmit for review.
Resubmission does not confirm the bill or remove validation warnings.

Bulk source intake accepts bounded CSV/XLSX/JSON with canonical columns. Preview
first. Staging retains source events for review and does not generate taxable
invoices from trips, payments or settlements. Import history is tenant scoped.

Reports continue to use confirmed canonical records and existing permissions.
Returns working papers remain subject to current approval/test restrictions.
No live GST portal filing or universal database connector is enabled.
