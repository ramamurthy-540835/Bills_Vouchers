## Scope and legal boundary

No GST rate, Decimal tax calculation, ITC eligibility, canonical confirmation, filing lock or government-submission rule was loosened. Incomplete or materially inconsistent bills remain reviewable against their original evidence. Auto Review remains optional and policy-gated. Existing skipped specialist scenarios are not silently marked passed.

## Evidence separation

Local mocked UI tests, application-service tests, actual isolated database/storage tests, actual model calls and owner-confirmed mail receipt are distinct acceptance levels. The final release report must identify blocked levels rather than treating a mock or SMTP acceptance as proof of live completion.
